## Development of IGO Telescope Control System 

I am currently writing the modern, linux-based telescope control system for the 2m IUCAA telescope at Girawali. As the legacy system runs on OpenVMS, a new modern software was necessary. My work is mostly to reverse-engineer the original software and establish communication between the different subsystems (Axis motors, Autoguider, Dome, etc.) with the TCS software. After completion, this software will enable remote, role-based access to observers to this telescope.

## Characterisation of HAWAII-xRG detectors for future instruments

I am also involved in characterizing HAWAII-2RG/4RG infrared detectors for the future planned instruments in the lab. The lab develops in-house built focal plane array controllers that enable complete control over these detectors to be used in different (fast, slow) speeds, sampling schemes and configurations. Using these controllers, I'm optimizing the read noise obtained by the whole electronics chain on room temperature and then in cryogenic arrangement. Next, we will use the detector to measure the important characteristics like persistence, linearity, dark current, etc.
