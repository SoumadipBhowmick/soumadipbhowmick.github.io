## Astronomy Club at IISERM

{{< figure class="figure" src="/IMG_20231029_012259.jpg" alt="Skywatch session">}}

I served as the convener of the Astronomy club at my university from 2023-24. In my tenure, we procured our first large-ish telescope (a 10" Dobsonian) used for public skywatch sessions and astrophotography. We also served the community by visiting secondary schools in the tricity and teaching the students about simple astronomy & physics concepts, showing sunspots from our telescopes, etc. 

---
## IOAA Orientation/Selection Camp

{{< figure class="figure" src="/IMG-20250606-WA0001.jpg" alt="OCSC Camp">}}

I was a teaching assistant for the Orientation cum Selection camp for [IOAA 2025](https://ioaa2025.in/). My sessions included assembly of telescopes, polar alignment and finding/observing targets on the sky. The selected students went on to win [4 gold medals](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2159394&reg=48&lang=2) for India in the Olympiad!
